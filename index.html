THE ATTENDANCE GATE — 100% Required for Award Program

An employee can be classified as an A-Player based on their evaluation score — but to receive the award and rewards, they must have:

✅ Zero absences for the month
✅ Zero tardiness for the month
Example:
Employee scores 94% → A-Player ✅
But had 1 absence → ⚠️ NOT award eligible

Rule: Classification is based on score. Award eligibility requires perfect attendance. No exceptions.
